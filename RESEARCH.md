# Dummy Surfers — Subway Surfers Fidelity Research (v4 renderer overhaul)

Research sources: Subway Surfers Wiki (Fandom) pages for Jake / Guard / Dog / Jetpack,
Google Play listing, gameplay guides, screenshot references. Collected 2026-09-02.

## 1. Why v1–v3 fell short
The game rendered a *pseudo-3D* scene: flat projected quads + ShapeRenderer chibis.
Real SS is a **true 3D scene**: perspective camera low behind the runner, volume on every
prop, soft warm sunlight, colorful trains with visible depth, smooth articulated run cycles.
No amount of 2D polish can reproduce that. → **v4 rebuilds the render layer on LibGDX
ModelBatch (real 3D meshes, lights, perspective camera)** while keeping the proven
gameplay state machine, economy, audio and UI systems.

## 2. Character: JACK (inspired-by Jake — 100% original art)
Wiki confirmation of the look we're channeling (not copying — all art below is ours):
- light-blue **denim vest** over **white hoodie** with **red undershirt**
- **red+white cap** (white front panel, red brim — worn backwards), brown hair
- **blue jeans**, white sneakers, spray-can kid energy
- SS chibi proportions: head ≈ 40% of height, big sneakers, expressive poses
→ Our JACK CharacterDef already matches this palette; v4 builds him as an
**articulated 3D rig** (head/torso/arms/legs/cap/pack) with a real run cycle.

## 3. The chase (Guard + Dog)
- Run starts with the Guard + dog on your heels; they fall back as you get clean.
- A glancing hit = **stumble** → guard closes into grab range (danger window).
- Second hit in the window (or a train) = **caught** → grab animation → game over.
- Wiki detail: the guard/dog can be hit by trains — they follow your lane.

## 4. Movement & world rules
- 3 lanes, swipe L/R = lane change, UP = jump, DOWN = roll.
- **Ramps** lean against trains → run up onto **train roofs** (roof-running).
- Trains: parked (dodge/climb), moving same-direction, and oncoming (horn + dodge).
- Barriers: low (jump), overhead/sign (roll under), full blockade (avoid).

## 5. Power-ups (Wiki + Play Store)
- **Jetpack** — fly above everything, coins in the sky
- **Coin Magnet** — pull coins from all lanes
- **Super Sneakers** — mega jump
- **x2 Multiplier** — doubles score
- **Hoverboard** — tap to ride, absorbs one crash (30s in SS, 15s here)

## 6. Scene anatomy (what the camera must see)
- gravel **ballast**, wooden **sleepers**, steel **rails** on 3 parallel tracks
- graffiti walls, catenary poles with wires, lamp posts, signals, billboards
- platform + shelter + waiting passengers (station segments)
- tunnels (arch ribs, dark interior), bridge girders
- city skyline + buildings, bright cyan sky, puffy clouds, sun glow
- warm sun (upper-left), soft blob shadows under everything

## 7. HUD / menus (SS language)
- top-left: gold **coin pill** (coin icon + count); top-right: score + missions
- chunky rounded white-outline text, bright green PLAY button, character cards
- game over: big "CAUGHT!" headline, score/coin tally, retry + menu

## 8. v4 renderer build order
1. ModelFactory (cached box/cylinder/textured models) 2. Scene3D camera+lights
3. Track (textured scrolling ballast/sleepers/rails) 4. 3D props (trains, barriers,
ramps) 5. JACK rig + run/jump/roll/lean/stumble 6. Guard + dog 7. HUD/menu polish
8. roof-running gameplay 9. endless visual QA loop (Xvfb screenshots).
